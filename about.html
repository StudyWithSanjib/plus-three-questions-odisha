About Us

Welcome to +3 PYQ & Syllabus Hub! Our platform is dedicated to providing free, easy access to previous year question papers (PYQs) and updated syllabus materials for Odisha university students under the NEP Model.

Our mission is to streamline exam preparation by bringing essential academic resources together in one clean, fast, and accessible hub — so students can spend less time searching and more time studying.

What We Offer

  • Previous year question papers organized by university, subject, semester, and year
  • NEP model syllabus for +3 Arts, Science, and Commerce streams
  • A simple way for students to request or contribute papers via our upload form

Who We Are

This platform is run independently by Study With Sanjib as an educational initiative to support Odisha's +3 student community. It is built and maintained voluntarily to make quality academic resources freely available.
